Frequently Asked Questions
What is the minimum budget to start advertising?
You can launch a CPM or push notification campaign with as little as $10. CPA campaigns require a minimum $50 deposit. Managed service campaigns start at $500/month. All budgets are fully controllable — daily caps and total limits are enforced to the cent.
How do you prevent bot traffic and click fraud?
Every impression and click is validated by our multi-layer anti-fraud system — IP reputation scoring, device fingerprinting, behavioural analysis, and publisher quality scores. Suspicious traffic is filtered before billing. We offer full transparency with raw traffic logs available on request.
Can I target specific countries or niches?
Yes. You can target by country, region, or city (195+ GEOs), device type, OS, browser, language, and publisher category (crypto, gaming, news, entertainment, etc.). You can also blacklist specific publishers or domains that underperform for your offer.
What creatives do I need to provide?
Requirements vary by format. Push notifications need a title (30 chars), message (45 chars), icon (192×192 px) and optional banner image. Banners need standard IAB sizes (JPG/PNG/GIF). CPA offers just need your landing page URL and payout details. Our managed service team can build creatives for you.
How quickly can my campaign go live?
Self-serve campaigns are reviewed and activated within 1–2 hours of submission. Managed campaigns require an onboarding call and go live within 24–48 hours. Push notification campaigns typically see first impressions within minutes of activation.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, crypto (BTC, USDT, ETH), and wire transfer for larger accounts. All transactions are processed securely. Indian advertisers can pay in INR with UPI or bank transfer — no international fees apply.
How do publishers get paid?
Publishers receive weekly payouts with a minimum withdrawal of just $5. We support UPI, bank transfer, PayPal, and crypto (BTC, USDT). Earnings are calculated on verified, fraud-free traffic only — you always see exactly what you earned and why.
What kind of traffic can I monetise as a publisher?
We accept websites, mobile apps, Telegram channels and bots, social media pages, and APK-based traffic. As long as your traffic is real and your content complies with our guidelines, you can monetise it across all our ad formats — push, popunder, in-page, native, and more.
